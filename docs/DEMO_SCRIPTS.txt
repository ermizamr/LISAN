SCENARIO 1: RURAL CLINIC TRIAGE
Languages: Amharic (Doctor) <-> Afaan Oromoo (Patient)
Mode: Conversation Mode
================================================================================

[TURN 1]
👨‍⚕️ DOCTOR (Amharic):
"እንደምን ዋልክ አባቴ፣ ዛሬ ምንህን ነው የሚያምህ?"
(Meaning: Good day father, what is bothering you today?)
--> Phone speaks Afaan Oromoo: "Akkam oolte abbaa koo, har'a maaltu si dhibe?"

[TURN 2]
👴 PATIENT (Afaan Oromoo):
"Baga nagaan jirtu doktoor. Guyyaa sadiif mataan na dhukkuba, akkasumas ho'i guddaan na qabeera."
(Meaning: Greetings doctor. For three days my head has been hurting, and I have had a severe fever.)
--> Phone speaks Amharic: "ጤና ይስጥልኝ ዶክተር። ለሦስት ቀናት ራሴን ያመኛል፣ እንዲሁም ከፍተኛ ትኩሳት ይዞኛል።"

[TURN 3]
👨‍⚕️ DOCTOR (Amharic):
"ብርድ ብርድ ማለት፣ ማቅለሽለሽ ወይም ሳል አለብህ?"
(Meaning: Do you feel chills, nausea, or coughing?)
--> Phone speaks Afaan Oromoo: "Dhaamochi, balaqqamsi ykn qufaan sitti jiraa?"

[TURN 4]
👴 PATIENT (Afaan Oromoo):
"Qufaan hin jiru, garuu galgala galgala qorri guddaan na qaba, qaamni koos ni hollata."
(Meaning: No cough, but every evening I feel intense shivering and my body shakes.)
--> Phone speaks Amharic: "ሳል የለም፣ ነገር ግን ማታ ማታ ብርቱ ብርድ ይይዘኛል፣ ሰውነቴም ይንቀጠቀጣል።"

[TURN 5]
👨‍⚕️ DOCTOR (Amharic):
"የወባ በሽታ መኖሩን ለማረጋገጥ አሁን ፈጣን የጣት ደም ምርመራ እናደርጋለን።"
(Meaning: To verify malaria, we will do a rapid finger-prick blood test right now.)
--> Phone speaks Afaan Oromoo: "Busaadhaa fi dhibee biraa mirkaneeffachuuf amma qorannoo dhiigaa ariifachiisaa siif goona."

[TURN 6]
👨‍⚕️ DOCTOR (Amharic):
"የምርመራው ውጤት መጥቷል፤ ሳንባህ ጤነኛ ነው፣ ነገር ግን ደምህ ውስጥ ወባ ተገኝቷል።"
(Meaning: The test results are in; your lungs are healthy, but malaria was found in your blood.)
--> Phone speaks Afaan Oromoo: "Firiin qorannoo dhufeera; sombi kee nagaadha, garuu dhiiga kee keessatti busaan argameera."

[TURN 7]
👨‍⚕️ DOCTOR (Amharic):
"ይህንን የወባ መድሃኒት ለሦስት ቀናት ከምግብ በኋላ በቀን ሁለት ጊዜ ውሰድ።"
(Meaning: Take this malaria medication for three days after meals, twice a day.)
--> Phone speaks Afaan Oromoo: "Qoricha busaa kana guyyoota sadiif nyaata booda guyyaatti yeroo lama fudhadhu."

[TURN 8]
👴 PATIENT (Afaan Oromoo):
"Galatoomi doktoor. Hojii maasii dhiisee bishaan danfe qofa dhuguun qabaa?"
(Meaning: Thank you doctor. Should I stop farm work and only drink boiled water?)
--> Phone speaks Amharic: "አመሰግናለሁ ዶክተር። የእርሻ ስራ ትቼ የፈላ ውሃ ብቻ መጠጣት አለብኝ?"

[TURN 9]
👨‍⚕️ DOCTOR (Amharic):
"አዎ፣ በደንብ እረፍት አድርግ፣ ብዙ ውሃ ጠጣ፣ ትኩሳቱ ካልቀነሰም ተመልሰህ ና።"
(Meaning: Yes, get plenty of rest, drink plenty of water, and come back if the fever doesn't drop.)
--> Phone speaks Afaan Oromoo: "Eeyyee, sirriitti boqodhu, bishaan baay'ee dhugi, yoo ho'i hin hir'anne immoo deebi'ii kottu."

[TURN 10]
👴 PATIENT (Afaan Oromoo):
"Fayyaa ta'aa doktoor, waaqayyo qoricha kana eebba haa godhu."
(Meaning: Stay healthy doctor, may God bless this medicine with healing.)
--> Phone speaks Amharic: "ጤና ይስጥልኝ ዶክተር፣ እግዚአብሔር ይህንን መድሃኒት ፈውስ ያድርገው።"

--> Phone speaks Tigrinya: "ሰላም! እዚ ጥንታዊ ናይ ሓወልቲ ግራናይት ኣዝዩ ዘደንቕ እዩ። ዕድሚኡ ክንደይ ዓመት እዩ?"

[TURN 2]
🇪🇹 GUIDE (Tigrinya):
"እንቋዕ ብደሓን መጻእኪ ናብ ጥንታዊት ከተማ ኣክሱም። እዚ ሓወልቲ ኣብ ራብዓይ ክፍለ ዘመን ብንጉሥ ኢዛና እዩ ተሃኒጹ።"

[TURN 4]
🇪🇹 GUIDE (Tigrinya):
"ካብ ኣርባዕተ ኪሎ ሜትር ርሕቀት ብዝነበሩ ሓርማዛትን ገዘፍቲ ዕንጨይትን ተጠቒሞም ብዝተዳለወ ጐደና ኣንከራፊፎም እዮም ኣምጺኦምዎ።"
